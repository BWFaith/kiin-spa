Good to know

Preparing for your visit

  • Skip waxing, scrubs or strong actives on the face for two days before.
  • Tell us about allergies, medications and recent procedures.
  • Wear light or no makeup if you can.
  • Peels may mean mild flaking for a few days. We will go over aftercare.

Facials & Skincare questions

How often should I get a facial?

Every four to six weeks suits most skin, in line with its natural renewal cycle.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. We choose gentle products and can patch test first.

Will I break out afterwards?

Some people see a small purge if skin was congested. It settles within a few days.

Can I wear makeup afterwards?

We suggest keeping skin bare for the rest of the day so it can rest.
