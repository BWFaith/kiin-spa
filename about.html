About K'iin

A salon and spa built around unhurried time, honest advice and genuine care.

Our story

Created by Eva Vera for people who rarely stop

K'iin Spa & Lounge was created by Eva Vera to give Plant City a place where care is done properly. The name is a nod to light and time, and that is the idea: a calm space where an hour belongs entirely to you.

Over the years the studio has grown from a single service into a full salon and spa, with specialists in massage, skin, hair, nails and lashes. What has not changed is the standard: welcome warmly, listen carefully, work with skill and send you home better than you arrived.
