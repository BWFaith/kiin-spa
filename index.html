Before you book

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, we work by appointment so every guest gets unhurried time. Book online or call (813) 345-1049.

How does the online quote work?

Choose your treatments and add-ons, and we show an instant estimate. When you confirm, a copy goes to your email and to our team, and we reach out to finalize your time.

Can I combine services in one visit?

Yes. Many guests pair a massage with a facial, or hair with nails. Add them all in the booking tool and we will plan the sequence.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available online through our gift card page.

What if I need to reschedule?

Call or message us as early as you can and we will find a new time.
