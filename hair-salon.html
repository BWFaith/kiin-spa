Your appointment

What to expect, step by step

  1. Consultation

    Photos, history and goals, plus a talk about maintenance.

  2. Wash and prep

    Scalp massage and products chosen for your hair.

  3. The work

    Cut, colour or styling done with care and checked as we go.

  4. Finish and aftercare

    Styled, photographed if you like, with home-care tips.

Good to know

Preparing for your visit

  • Bring inspiration photos. They help more than words.
  • Colour appointments run long, so plan your day around them.
  • For braids, arrive with hair washed, detangled and blow-dried unless told otherwise.
  • Tell us about previous colour or chemical services before we start.

Hair Salon questions

How do I choose a colour?

Bring photos and we will talk about what suits your skin tone and upkeep.

How long do braids take?

Between three and six hours, depending on size and length. We will give you an estimate when booking.

Can you fix colour done elsewhere?

Often, yes. Book a consultation and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Do you do kids' styles?

Please call us and we will help find a time that suits.
